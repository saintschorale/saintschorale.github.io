Laura Osborn joined the Arizona Saints Chorale in 2021. She started playing
piano at age 5 and has been in choirs for over 10 years.

She currently teaches elementary school and loves helping kids learn to read.
Laura enjoys her free time collecting creative hobbies and exploring nature with
her husband, Glenn.
